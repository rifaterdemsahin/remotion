# Thumbnail Design Rules

## Theme

Futuristic digital illustration featuring a female figure wearing a sleek VR/AR headset and tech suit.

## Composition

- **Center**: Female figure with arms spread wide open in an empowering pose.
- **Background**: Dark blue-to-black gradient resembling a neon-lit cityscape.
- **Surrounding**: 6-8 glowing circular tech icons arranged symmetrically around the body.

## Icons

- AI/intelligence
- Navigation/steering wheel
- Growth chart/analytics
- Rocket/innovation
- Cloud computing
- Data/network connections
- **Style**: Orange/amber outer ring with cyan/turquoise inner glow and connecting circuit lines.

## Lighting & Effects

- Bright cyan and turquoise energy beams/light rays from the center.
- Sense of power and connectivity.
- Digital cityscape silhouette in soft-focus background.
- Subtle digital grid patterns, lens flares, and particle effects.

## Text Elements

- **Box**: Horizontal blue neon box at the bottom.
- **Main Text**: "FUTURE-PROOF YOUR CAREER" (Bold white sans-serif).
- **Subtitle**: "Mastering the AI Revolution" (Smaller text below).

## Color Palette

- Deep navy and dark teal backgrounds.
- Bright cyan and turquoise accents.
- Warm orange/amber for icon rings.
- White text.
- Purple to orange gradient border frame on outer edges.

## Style

Modern, professional, tech-forward.
