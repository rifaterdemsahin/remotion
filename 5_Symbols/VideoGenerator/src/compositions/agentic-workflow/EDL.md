## Audio Specifications

- **Background Music**: Low-fi beats or tech-ambient.
  - **Ducking**: -15dB during speech. Fade out completely at end.
- **SFX**:
  - *Whoosh* on transitions.
  - *Keyboard clack* on code appearance.
  - *Notification ping* on Telegram scene.
  - *Data beep* on counter increment.

## Visual Notes & Pacing

- **Color Grading**:

  - **A-Roll**: High contrast, slightly cool tone (Blue/Grey) for professionalism.
  - **B-Roll (Dashboard)**: Vibrant, neon accents (Cyan/Magenta) to emphasize "Tech/AI". Dark mode UI.
  - **Warning/Problem Sections**: desaturated with Red accents.
- **Transitions**:

  - **Standard**: Cross Dissolve (0.5s) for smooth narrative flow.
  - **Section Breaks**: Glitch effect or quick "Zoom Thru" transition (0.2s) when moving from A-Roll to Screen Share.
  - **Text**: Kinetic entry (Spring physics), clean exit (Fade).
- **Pacing**:

  - **Intro**: Fast, aggressive cuts to match "Ferrari" energy.
  - **Problem Statement**: Slow, deliberate zoom-ins on key text.
  - **Solution/Demo**: Rhythmic, matching the beat of background music. Cut on action (mouse click, screen change).
